Band and settings

On the two list pages, the band's name replaces the GigBuddy wordmark on the left of the header. Tapping it opens a sheet with the band list and Export, which moves off the foot of the songs page. Song, gig and playing screens have a back link there instead, so you can't switch band mid-gig by accident. Choosing a band switches straight away, with no Save, and Done closes the sheet.

Sign in

Seen rarely, but it's the first screen on a new phone.

Songs

The library, a song, and adding, editing and deleting. If you try to delete a song that's in a gig's setlist, a dialog lists the gigs instead of offering Delete, as the app does now.

Gigs

Gigs, a gig's setlist, and the sheets for adding a gig, editing it and editing its setlist, including the message when a line doesn't match a song in the library.

Playing a gig

A full screen, with nothing over the chart. Shown in the middle of a set with Next being pressed, at the start and at the end. The "Screen may sleep" line appears only when the phone won't stay awake.

Laptop

The same app at 1024px wide. Content stays in a 40rem column, sheets slide in from the right, and the footer regions stay a sensible width instead of stretching. These frames scroll sideways on a phone.

The accents all pass WCAG AA as text in both modes. Light mode uses a darker shade of each, and the brass is darker than today's. Oxblood sits close to the Delete red, so if you choose it, the Delete red should move towards orange.
